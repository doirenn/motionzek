Everything You Need to Know

Video editing costs vary based on project duration, raw footage complexity, pacing style, motion graphics, and audio treatment. Individual standalone projects start from $250 USD, while short-form retainer packs start from $850 USD per month.

Every project receives a custom transparent proposal after reviewing your raw footage, timeline expectations, and reference videos. There are no hidden fees.

Post-production is the complete process of refining recorded footage into a polished final video. It encompasses video assembly, audio cleaning, sound design, color grading, title design, motion graphics, and exporting to master formats.

Unlike basic cutting, professional post-production elevates narrative flow, reinforces visual identity, and ensures structural compliance across YouTube, broadcast, or social media ad platforms.

Video editing focuses on cutting, assembling, and pacing real camera footage. Motion graphics involves designing animated graphic assets, 2D kinetic typography, 3D elements, lower thirds, and visual effects built from scratch.

At Motionzek, both disciplines are combined seamlessly so your video footage includes custom graphic callouts, animated logos, and visual hooks.

A standard 10 to 15-minute YouTube video typically takes 3 to 5 business days for first-draft delivery. Complex documentary-style edits with extensive B-roll and motion graphics take 5 to 7 business days.

Rush turnarounds can be coordinated depending on current studio availability and project specifications.

You need to provide raw camera footage, audio recordings, script or outline, brand assets (logo, font files, color guidelines), and 1 to 2 visual reference links.

You can upload raw files via Google Drive, Dropbox, or Frame.io Transfer.

Standard single projects include 2 rounds of revision feedback. Retainer packs include unlimited minor adjustments to fine-tune pacing and subtitles.

All reviews take place online via Frame.io, allowing you to leave timestamped notes directly on the video screen.
